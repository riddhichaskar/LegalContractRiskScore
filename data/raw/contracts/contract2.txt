EMPLOYMENT CONTRACT

This Employment Contract is made between Employer and Employee.

1. SALARY  
The Employee shall receive a fixed monthly salary. Delayed attendance may result in deductions.

2. TERMINATION  
Either party may terminate this agreement with a 30-day written notice. Immediate termination may occur in case of misconduct.

3. PENALTIES  
A penalty of ₹50,000 will be imposed if the Employee leaves before completing the minimum contract period.

4. LIABILITY  
The Employer shall not be responsible for any personal losses or damages incurred during employment.

5. DISPUTE RESOLUTION  
All disputes shall be resolved through binding arbitration.
